INSTALLATION
Preparatory steps must be completed by the electrician before the light is installed, see Figure 1.

1.Ensure that the electrical system of the pool conforms with the reguirements of the current all local codes and ordinances.

- The junction box, or the low voltage transformer for 12 volt models, is located at least 120CM from the edge of the pool, see Figure 1.

- The mounting bracket must be properly installed so that the top edge of the Underwater Light's lens is at least 45CM below the surface of the water in the pool, see Figure 1.
2. Locate the position on a vertical wall where light is to be installed. The top of the light lens must be 45CM below normal water level, see Figure 1.

3. Connect conduit to the midst of Mounting Spacer (left of Bracket), see Figure 2.

CAUTION: Finish area surrounding Mounting Spacer MUST be flat and flush with the face of the Mounting Spacer, this ensures a snug fit between light and wall, see Figure 3.B.
Perform the following steps after the electrical system reguirements are met.

4. Wrap a length of the cord up to a maximum of eight(8) feet long on the back of the light assembly. This extra cord allows you to bring the light out of the pool for relamping and
servicing.

5._Connect cord electrical wires at Junction box, through Conduit, be careful not to pull the three (3) inches of slack cord at the light through the conduit when connecting the
wires.

6. Connect all wires to the 'corresponding circuit wires in the junction box and feed paraffin.

7. Secure the junction box cover in place.

8. Before operating the light for more than 10 seconds fill the pool until the Underwater Light is completely submerged in water. To check for proper operation turn on the main
switch or circuit breaker as well as the switch that operates the Underwater Light itself.

9. Rotating Locking System ensures light is secured to bracket, see Figure 4.

C. Winterizing: Light should be left in place for winterization.

Never operate this Underwater Light for more than 10 seconds unless it is totally submerged in water. Without total submersion, the fight assembliy will get extremely hot, which
May result in serious injury to pool users, installers, or bystanders, or in damage to property.
